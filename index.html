Shop rules (pinned above the bench)

  1. Grain runs with the length. Arguing with it is how tear-out is born.
  2. Wood moves. Design for it, or it will redesign for you.
  3. Sharp tools are safe tools. Dull ones are just loud.
  4. Every scrap is a future spline, wedge or coaster.

    Finishing in one breath

    Sand up through the grits (120 → 150 → 180), raise the grain with a damp cloth, knock it back with 220, dust off, test on an offcut, then thin coats beat thick ones every single time. Blotchy pine or cherry? A wash coat of dewaxed shellac first.

    The rosewood marimba

    Sheesham bars over brass resonators. Click, tap, or use your keyboard: Z–M lower octave, Q–U upper, S D G H J and 2 3 5 6 7 for sharps, I for high C.

    Pro tip: marimba bars are thinned under the middle so the 4th harmonic rings in tune. Your bars are thinned with math.

    Woodcraft Atelier is a love letter to sawdust: a free, fully client-side workshop with no sign-up, no trackers and no API keys. Every wood surface here is generated procedurally — fBm noise for the growth rings, stretched noise for fibres and pores, knots that bend the rings around them, and SVG turbulence for the fine texture.
